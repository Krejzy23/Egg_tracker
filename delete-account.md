# Delete Account Request

If you want to delete your Egg Tracker account and all associated data, you can request account deletion by contacting us.

## How to request account deletion

Send an email to:

**ales.krejzl@gmail.com**

with the subject:

**Delete my Egg Tracker account**

Please include the email address you used to register in the app.

## What data will be deleted

After receiving your request, we will delete:

- your user account
- your saved egg entries
- your chicken count data
- any app data associated with your account

## Data retention

Account deletion requests are processed manually.

We will delete your account and associated data within **30 days** of receiving your request.
